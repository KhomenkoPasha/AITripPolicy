Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

AITripPlanner (Android package: khom.pavlo.aitripplanner) is provided by Pavlo Khomenko. This policy explains how the app and its supporting services process personal data. For privacy questions and requests, contact hompavlik@gmail.com.

2. Account and travel data

When you register, we process your name, email address and password to create and protect your account. The server stores a password hash, user identifier and session information. Travel requests include the destination, trip duration, interests, budget preference, pace, travel mode, language and any text you enter. We use them to generate itineraries and recommendations. Saved routes, places, favorites and visited-place marks are stored on your device and, when you use account synchronization, on our server.

3. AI-generated itineraries

To generate a route, our server sends your travel request and planning preferences to OpenAI. Your account password is not included in the itinerary prompt. Do not enter sensitive personal information or information about other people that is unnecessary for planning. AI recommendations can be inaccurate; check travel details before relying on them.

4. Location, maps and photos

If you allow location access, the app can show your current position on the map while you use the map feature. You can refuse or revoke approximate or precise location access in Android settings. The app does not provide background location tracking. Map tile requests reveal the viewed map area and network information to the map provider. Destination coordinates and visited-place marks are part of saved trip data.

Photos you choose to attach to places are copied into the app’s local storage. These personal photo files are not included in the app’s trip synchronization. Android backup may copy app data according to your device and backup settings. Photos of attractions supplied by external services are separate from your own photos.

5. Technical information and purposes

Our server and infrastructure providers process network and service information, such as IP addresses, request times, request paths, response status and error information, to deliver the service, troubleshoot failures and protect it from abuse. We also process the content of messages you send to support. The app does not contain advertising SDKs or use an advertising identifier for ad targeting. We do not sell personal data.

The Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand screen visits, sessions and app usage. Analytics processes app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and approximate location derived from masked IP addresses. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash stack traces, device and app state, installation identifiers and screen names to diagnose failures. We do not attach your name, email, password, trip text or coordinates to our custom analytics events or crash-report fields.

6. Service providers and external links

We use Render to host the backend and Neon to store account and synchronized trip data; OpenAI for itinerary generation; Photon (komoot) for city search; OpenStreetMap services for Android map tiles; and Google Places for attraction photos and related place lookups. Providers receive the information needed for the particular request, including network information where your device connects directly. Opening an external map or a place’s website sends the selected destination or opens that provider’s service, which has its own privacy practices. This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes web access information. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers to this website.

Data may be processed outside your country by these service providers. See their policies: Render, Neon, Firebase, OpenAI, komoot, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Google and GitHub.

7. Retention, security and deletion

Account and synchronized trip data are retained to provide your account and saved-trip features until you request their deletion. Technical information is retained for operational and security needs, and limited records may need to be retained to meet applicable legal requirements. If an exception affects your deletion request, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period in our response. Independent service providers may retain data under their own policies.

Connections to our backend use HTTPS, and passwords are stored as hashes. No system can guarantee absolute security. Removing the app or clearing its storage removes local app data but does not delete your server account. You can request account and associated server-data deletion through the account deletion page. You can also ask to delete particular data without closing the whole account.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use “Continue offline” for available local features; AI generation and account synchronization require sign-in. You can control location and photo access through your device settings. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your data, restriction of processing, or object to processing, and complain to a data protection authority. Contact the email above; we may need to verify ownership of the account. Where consent is required, you can withdraw it. Processing needed to deliver the features you request and maintain service security may have other applicable legal grounds.

9. Age and policy changes

AITripPlanner is intended for users aged 13 and above. Where local law requires a higher age or a parent’s permission, those requirements apply. If a child below the applicable age has provided personal data, contact us to request removal. We update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above.
