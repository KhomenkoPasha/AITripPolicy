Delete your account and data

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Request deletion of your AITripPlanner account and the data associated with it.

1. How to request deletion

Send an email from the address used for your AITripPlanner account to hompavlik@gmail.com with the subject AITripPlanner account deletion. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. If you want only specific data removed, identify it in the message.

2. What will be deleted

The request covers your account profile (name, email and user identifier), authentication records and synchronized trips, including saved places, favorites and visited-place marks. We may ask you to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password. Requests are reviewed manually; opening the email link does not submit a request or immediately delete data.

3. Retention exceptions

Data required by applicable law or necessary to resolve an outstanding security issue may need to be retained. If this applies, we will explain what is retained, why, and the applicable period when responding. Removing the app alone does not delete the server account.

4. Local data and backups

Your personal photos and other local app data are stored on your device. Remove them in the app or clear the app’s storage in Android settings. Copies in your device backup are controlled through your backup provider’s settings.
